Everything you need to make your next pair an easy decision.

How do I place an order?

Add a pair to your bag, review your details at checkout, then choose your preferred payment method. We'll confirm the order by phone or email.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Nairobi and can arrange shipping to the rest of Kenya. Nairobi delivery is complimentary for orders over KSh 10,000.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually prepared within one business day. Nairobi deliveries arrive in 1–2 business days; upcountry delivery takes 3–5 business days.

Can I return or exchange a pair?

Yes. Contact us within 7 days of delivery. Items should be unworn, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

How do I find my size?

Choose your usual size and contact us if you are between sizes. Our team can help compare measurements across brands before you order.

How can I contact support?

Call +254 735 375 762 or email hello@jonajshoepalace.com. We're available Monday to Saturday, 9:00–18:00.
